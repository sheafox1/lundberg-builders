How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. Initial phone call

A project consultant learns the scope, wants and needs, timeline and goals.

2. Meet and visit the site

We meet at our office or your home, then visit the site to review orientation, terrain and neighboring structures.

3. Preliminary plans

We check local building, zoning and critical area rules, then prepare preliminary floor plans and elevations.

4. Selections, budget and permits

We review product selections, set the final construction management budget and begin the permit process.
